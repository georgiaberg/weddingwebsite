Our Story

portrait of georgia & parsa

Parsa and I met in the fall of 2021 in Waterloo.

Our first date was in a lecture hall on the fourth floor of the Mathematics and Computing building. We wanted to see The Rocky Horror Picture Show at the Princess, but every ticket was sold out — so we dressed up anyway and watched it together where we were.

We have been inseparable ever since. Six years later we would love you with us in Vaughan, for a Kurdish-Canadian evening of dinner, dancing and far too many photographs.
